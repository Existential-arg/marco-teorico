Presenting the Existential Model

What do we mean by existential?

When we speak of the existential, we mean human existence as the condition of being alive and aware of it. We understand existence as the unfolding of human life through its history, including every experience, action and circumstance we face. Existence spans biological, psychological, social and cultural aspects, and human beings are unique in their ability to build their lives around a personal and social project they have chosen.

It is important to clarify that, although we use the term "existential", we do not subscribe to existentialism as a philosophical school. That school, developed in the 20th century, explored themes such as freedom, responsibility and the search for meaning in the absence of any intrinsic meaning in the world. While we share some of its points, we move away from certain aspects, such as existential pessimism, extreme individualism and philosophical abstraction.

For us, the existential implies an integrative and positive view of human life, centered on authenticity and purpose.
